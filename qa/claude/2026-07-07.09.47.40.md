Against your pass bar (K3 nonrep >= K0 + 0.15; >=2 seeds; not order-dependent):

## Two positives that DO survive the hardening

- **(a) The compact-QA consolidation is genuinely gentle.** With zero old replay it still holds ~0.63-0.74
  non-replayed old paraphrase (vs naive_cpt 0.0). Sequential QA-target consolidation from M_{t-1} is far
  less destructive than raw continued-PT. But this is a *consolidation-objective* effect, not a
  replay-coverage effect, and I can't tell from this run whether the ~0.65 floor is real-text shared
  structure or just objective gentleness. To separate them I'd need a same-objective synthetic control
  (does gentle compact-QA consolidation on *independent* synthetic facts also hold ~0.65 non-replayed, or
  collapse to ~0?). If synthetic collapses and real holds, THAT is the clean redundancy claim — the pilot
  was measuring the right intuition with the wrong (fresh-inflated) estimator.

- **(b) The neutral base-KL anchor mildly HURTS old-fact retention.** k0_noanchor > k0 on non-replayed old
  para in both seeds (+0.10, +0.125) and on final para. The anchor pulls M toward the base prior and erodes
  learned facts. Counterintuitive but consistent across seeds — the anchor is a net negative on this metric.
