# Brainstorm: the "recognition without recall" wall — knowledge bound to the exact question form.

Open discussion, bring your own ideas + push back hard. This is the phenomenon R48 + R49a (shakedown,
2-seed confirm running) jointly expose, and I think it reframes the WHOLE self-replay program. Files:
`docs/cloud_results/r48_census_decision.*`, `docs/cloud_results/r49a_shakedown.*`, `s3/recall_ladder.py`.

## The phenomenon (precise)
A 0.5B model INTERNALIZES obscure census facts — it answers them closed-book when given the exact/paraphrased
question (R49a L5 = 1.0; R48 commit = full; stored replay protects them). But:
- **R48**: free self-generation NEVER surfaces them (coverage U_admit=0; it dreams only high-prior pretrained
  facts — capitals, continents). recovery of the stored-replay retention gap = 0.
- **R49a ladder** (correct recall of a THREATENED fact vs cue strength): L0_free / L1_fixed / L2_domain = 0;
  **L3_entity = 0** (it generates a lexically-matching question but ANSWERS IT WRONG, terr≈1.0); **L4 =
  answer-redacted near-complete question = 0.077** (again: it completes the question but answers its own
  completion wrong); only **L5 = full question = 1.0**.

So the fact is stored as a TIGHT (question-surface → answer) mapping — RECOGNITION memory — not as an
addressable/queryable proposition the model can RECALL or GENERATE. The address is ~the whole cue. The model
can't even answer a question it ITSELF generated about the right entity. This is a reversal-curse-class
binding (A→B trained, B→A / "tell me about A" / free recall all fail), but sharper: even a near-complete cue
fails because the model's reconstruction drifts off the exact stored form.

## Why this reframes self-replay
Self-replay (rehearsal-free retention) REQUIRES the model to RECALL its own old facts to regenerate targets.
But the model has recognition, not recall. So:
- rehearsal-free self-replay may be **impossible at this scale by construction** — you can't extract what is
  only recognition-addressable without supplying the cue (= stored replay, which is NOT rehearsal-free).
- The fork: (a) accept stored cues (not rehearsal-free), or **(b) fix ACQUISITION so facts are written in a
  RECALL-able / self-addressable form in the first place.** I think (b) is the real frontier and it moves the
  problem UPSTREAM from "how to generate" to "how to encode."

## My proposed mechanisms (attack encoding, not retrieval)

1. **Multi-view / bidirectional acquisition (R50 candidate).** At write time, train each new fact in MANY
   forms from the CURRENT stream (legitimate — it's the new data): forward Q→A, reverse A→Q, cloze, entity→
   proposition ("Facts about X: …"), and crucially the FREE-GENERATION direction ("Tell me a fact you know. →
   <this fact>"). Hypothesis: this converts a recognition-only encoding into a recall-able one, so the model's
   OWN free/entity-cued generation surfaces the fact — raising R49a L0/L3 coverage from ~0. Then self-replay
   becomes possible because recall works. Cheap to test: re-run the ladder where acquisition includes the
   reverse+free-gen directions, measure ΔL0/ΔL3. This is the known reversal-curse mitigation (augment
   directions/paraphrases) repurposed as an ADDRESSABILITY mechanism, and it's the highest-leverage shot.

2. **Entity-anchored addressing (reuse R46/R47 bridge geometry).** Free generation dreams high-prior facts
   because the entity's pretrained prior dominates the generative distribution. Attach the new fact to the
   entity's existing representation: train h("tell me about <entity>") / h("<entity> is known for") to move
   toward the new proposition (a successor-feature/attractor loss like R46, but for ADDRESSING not
   composition). The entity becomes a self-address for the fact. Ties R46's one positive to this wall.

3. **A "recall head" / free-generation objective co-trained at acquisition** (your last-round #1, sharpened):
   train the fact to be emittable from a small fixed set of learned latent "recall seeds" that live in
   weights; sleep = sample seeds → regenerate → consolidate. The address is internal, not an item ledger.

## Where I want you to push / think
1. **Is (b) — acquisition-side recall-able encoding — right, or am I wrong that self-replay needs recall?**
   Counter-argument to myself: maybe rehearsal-free retention never needed the model to RE-GENERATE old
   facts at all — maybe the right rehearsal-free mechanism protects the WEIGHTS/subspace directly (null-space,
   EWC, interference-aware writes) without any recall. We negatively tested those (R36/R43) but not to
   exhaustion. So is the recognition-vs-recall wall a reason to (i) fix encoding for self-replay, or (ii)
   ABANDON self-replay and return to weight-space protection with a sharper interference mechanism? Which
   fork has the higher ceiling toward the ultimate goal?
2. **Multi-view acquisition risk**: does training reverse+free-gen directions COST retention/interference
   (more updates per fact = more forgetting of others), and does it even work at 0.5B, or is free recall a
   capability that only emerges at larger scale (in which case this is a scale story, not a mechanism story)?
   Cheap discriminator: run the R49a ladder on a 3B base with the SAME facts — if 3B's L0/L3 self-surfacing
   is materially >0 while 0.5B is 0, recall is scale-gated and multi-view acquisition may be unnecessary at
   scale. Worth doing that scale-probe BEFORE building multi-view?
3. **Deeper reframe I might be missing**: is "recognition without recall" actually the CORRECT and desirable
   state for a knowledge store (retrieval is the LM's job when cued), and the real bug is that we DEMANDED
   self-generation at all? I.e., is the honest end-state "continual learning needs a small stored cue-set
   (compact question stubs), which is O(facts) but tiny, and TRUE rehearsal-free is a mirage at this scale"?
   Push back on my instinct that (b) is buildable — maybe the ultimate-goal-honest answer is a minimal cue
   ledger + interference-aware weight protection, not self-recall.

Give me your open read: rank the forks (fix-encoding vs abandon-for-weight-protection vs scale-probe vs
accept-minimal-cue-ledger), and brainstorm any mechanism I haven't named. Don't defer — tell me where the
recognition/recall reframe is wrong or where the highest-ceiling path actually is.
