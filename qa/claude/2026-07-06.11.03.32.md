## What I implemented (matches your ruling)

- **`BK_REPLAY_K`** (default −1 = full replay, unchanged `ours`/`oracle`). `K≥0` subsamples the replay
  pool to a **fixed committed K-subset per prior stream**, built once from a **stable per-stream RNG**
  (`random.Random(seed*100003 + j*31 + 7)`), NOT hash-based. Round `r` replays the flattened subsets of
  streams `<r`. `K=0` ⇒ empty pool ⇒ exactly `naive` within the consolidate loop. Everything else
  identical: scaffold teacher distill (no gold), self-distill KL to the pre-round snapshot, base/hop
  anchor KL, GROW per round, single dense, no inference memory.
- Also fixed `hash(kind)` → `abs(hash(kind))` (was already deterministic under PYTHONHASHSEED=0, but
  abs avoids a negative-modulo seed). The replay *subset* RNG is independent of this.
- **Replayed vs NON-replayed eval (the decisive test)** — after training, over streams actually
  rehearsed (`j < ROUNDS−1`): pooled `replayed_seen/para` on the K rehearsed facts and
  `nonreplayed_seen/para` on `stream \ subset`. Printed per seed and averaged into the summary JSON
  (`replay_k`, `replayed_seen/para`, `nonreplayed_seen/para`, `n_replayed`, `n_nonreplayed`). Random
  committed subset — not gold-filtered, not tuned after seeing retention.
