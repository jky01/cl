proposer training. Arms (all history-free; hidden gold = offline audit only; M_prev = acquired base holder):
- `passive_base`: M_prev few-shot L0/L1 (the R48 floor).
- `instruct_static`: FROZEN Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct EXTERNAL proposer via its NATIVE chat template (apply_chat_

  artifact" question. (I did NOT switch the holder to instruct — base stays the knowledge holder.)
- `shadow_contrastive`: contrastive-decode from M_prev steered by `logp_M + λ(logp_M − logp_S_real)`,
  restricted to M_prev top-p 0.95 support (fluency), λ=1. S_real = 60-step real future write.
- `wrong_shadow_contrastive`: same vs S_wrong (shadow trained on SHUFFLED answers) — must lose ≥half coverage.
Every hit requires: signature-match to an answerable old fact AND M_prev answers it right AND proposition-
equivalent (3B judge `mp.judge_equiv`) AND positive acquired-lift `logp_M(a) − logp_base(a) > 0`. Raw rows saved.

Gates in `decide()`: instruct_policy_signal = cov_correct_ans ≥0.15 & > passive, both seeds; shadow_signal =
cov_ans ≥0.15 & ≥2× passive & ≥2× wrong-shadow, both seeds. Threatened is underpowered on census (n~9) so I
made ANSWERABLE cov the primary readout and report threatened secondary.
