## Two things I want you to sanity-check

1. **Multi-fact-per-passage extraction risk.** In shared_templates, a passage can contain 2-3 "capital of X
   is Y" sentences with different invented X/Y. RAG screening must disambiguate the right one for a 0.5B model
   on lookalike invented names. I rely on the RAG-answerable screen to DROP facts the model can't extract, so
   survivors are cleanly answerable — but if the screen is too lossy, few survive (over-select 2x, 7 cand/art
   -> keep 5, should hold). Acceptable, or do you want 1 fact/passage to remove intra-passage interference
   (at the cost of diverging from R38B's multi-QA-per-article structure)? I lean keep multi (matches R38B).
2. **Answer-string uniqueness across corpus** is enforced (global `used` set covers both subjects and
   answers), so no answer leaks between facts. Confirm that's the independence you meant.
