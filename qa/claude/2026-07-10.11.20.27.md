- **Stage 1 `generate_candidates(M, N, seed)`**: M generates N raw (question, sampled_answer) from a FIXED
  generic 3-shot seed (public-prior facts: France/Shakespeare/gold — O(1), NOT old-stream items). Sampled
  (temp 0.9, top_p 0.95). Parses "…?\nAnswer: …". NO old qids/titles/subjects ever passed in.
- **Stage 2 `admit_candidates`**: canonical answer a* = M's GREEDY closed-book answer to q; admit iff M's
  answer to a held-out paraphrase of q EQUALS a* (cross-view consensus) AND per-token gold logprob >=
  ADMIT_MARGIN. Freeze a* as the CE target. (Paraphrase via the 3B, data-prep, then freed.)
- **Stage 3 `make_shadow` + `fragility_scores`**: ONE shared shadow = M copy + SHADOW_STEPS(60) LM-only
  micro-update on the CURRENT stream (no old replay, no anchor). Fragility = per-token gold-answer bits
  INCREASE M->shadow on admitted candidates. `self_passive_fragile_B` = top-B by that; `self_passive_random_B`
  = uniform B from the SAME admitted pool + SAME frozen targets.
- **Stage 4 `audit_coverage` (OFFLINE, post-selection, never feeds back)**: match generated candidate to old
  gold iff normalized ANSWER equality AND question subject-token Jaccard>=0.34. Emits C_raw, C_admit,
  yield, dup_rate, per-AGE coverage, and the matched-gold-qid set (-> generated-vs-never-generated old-EM split).
- **Arms**: `no_replay_compute_matched` (empty pool + LM filler to match FLOPs) | `stored_random_B`
  (`select_budget(committed,"random",B)`) | `self_passive_random_B` | `self_passive_fragile_B`. B=0.5*|committed|.
- **`verdict()`**: Delta_B = stored - no_replay; recovery = (self_random - no_replay)/Delta_B; fragility_gain
  = fragile - random. Gates: Delta_B>=0.10, recovery>=0.50, fragility>=+0.05. per-seed rows persisted.
- **Ledger**: self arms `persistent_old_items=0`; gen_tokens logged.
