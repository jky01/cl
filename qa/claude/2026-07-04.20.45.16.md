## Time-budget reality + two honest issues with jumping straight to grokking

~2.3h remain in the current autonomous window. Two problems with launching the grokking probe (candidate A) right now:

1. **Entity-scale feasibility.** Composition grokking in the literature (Grokked Transformers, 2024) needs *thousands* of entities and long past-overfitting training. Our single-token answer pool is only ~44 (`single_tok_names`), which is far too small for grokking to plausibly emerge — a null result would be uninterpretable (scale-limited, not depth-limited). We'd first need a **large single-token entity pool** (harvest all-alpha single-token ids straight from the vocab → thousands) and likely a **2-relation** design (r1∘r2) so in-distribution held-out (A,C) pairs reuse seen nodes. That's a non-trivial data-pipeline build.
2. **Runtime.** The R33 run was slow (~2.5h/2 seeds) mostly because `train_memory` is retrained *per arm per stream*. Grokking needs long training (10k–100k steps); it won't finish meaningfully in 2h.

## Proposal for THIS remaining window: bulletproof R33 (certain value), defer grokking

Rather than start a finicky multi-hour grokking run that likely won't converge before the window closes, I propose spending the remaining time making the *proven, comparative* result airtight:

1. **Efficiency refactor (also speeds everything later):** train the scaffold **once per (seed, stream)** and share it across ours/naive/loramerge/extmem, instead of retraining per arm. ~4× fewer scaffold trainings → a 3-seed + extra-arm rerun becomes ~1–1.5h.
2. **Add the two arms you flagged as second-tier but high-value:**
   - **replay-with-gold oracle** (upper bound; sequential replay but old-stream gold) — if ours≈oracle, that's a strong result.
   - **EWC / diagonal-Fisher** on the same trainable layers, λ∈{10,100,1000} — the canonical CL regularizer baseline, to show replay beats not just naive but a real anti-forgetting method.
3. **Complete the metric set:** report **para** (not just seen) and the full compute/params/vram/flag JSON at **3 seeds**, so the bakeoff table is publication-grade.

Then grokking (candidate A) becomes the **first task of the next fresh window**, where I'll build the large-vocab 2-relation in-distribution design + your hard kill criteria (train-fit<0.95 in 2k→stop; held-out<0.05 after fit+5k→stop; growth must cut onset ≥30% or lift ceiling ≥0.10 at matched compute).
