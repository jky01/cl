## I nearly pivoted to A on a confounded number. I didn't. Here's why.

Your gate said: CE `energy-occ ≈ 0.05` ⇒ stop OGD, pivot to A. The CE smoke gave **0.058**, which
looked like a pivot trigger. But before pivoting I checked whether that low occupancy was **real
near-orthogonality** or a **rank/collection artifact** of the tiny smoke (2 streams × 20 facts →
only `ncol=27` committed-correct dirs collected, rank capped at 48). Critically evaluating rather than
taking the smoke at face value changed the answer.

**Rank/spec scan (ogd, ce_gold):**

| config                              | rank | ncol | energy-occ(r1) | upd-leak |
|-------------------------------------|------|------|----------------|----------|
| smoke (2×20, 60 steps)              | 48   | 27   | **0.058**      | 0.0045   |
| rank64 (2×80, 400 steps)            | 64   | 80   | **0.179**      | 0.0018   |
| rank96 (2×160)                      | 96   | —    | OOM at merge   | —        |
| rank160 (2×160)                     | 160  | —    | OOM at collect | —        |
| nswrite reference (per-module)      | ~320 | —    | **0.906**      | —        |

Two facts:
1. **The smoke under-tested OGD.** With a properly specified basis (`ncol=80`, rank 64), CE occupancy
   is **0.179 — 3× the smoke's 0.058**, and *materially above your ≥0.15 "run the full pilot"
   threshold.* The 0.058 was starvation (27 dirs), not near-orthogonality. Also note rank-48 occ 0.058
   is already **~36,000× a random rank-48 subspace** (48/29.8M ≈ 1.6e-6), so there is strong structure;
   it just needs enough rank/dirs to show.
2. **Flattened OGD is memory-bound.** rank 96 and 160 OOM on 32 GB — the per-stream merge
   `C=[P≈29.8M, R_old+kper]` is O(P·rank) and doubles transiently. So OGD *cannot* cheaply reach
   nswrite's factorized coverage (0.906) — a structural asymmetry (global low-rank vs per-module).
   But at the affordable rank 64 it now clears your pilot gate, so the pilot is warranted.
