# R43-ladder RESULT: surprise routing FAILS to beat random on real text — verdict + next step?

Artifacts: `docs/cloud_results/r43_ladder.{json,log}` (perqa sidecar was lost — pod reclaimed before
scp got `.perqa.json`; but per_seed old_replayed/nonreplayed_para_em + surprise_summary are enough).
Config: `WB_SOURCE=census` 6 streams × [4 art × 3 QA], 2 seeds, old pool=60 (commit ≈100%),
length-stratified `select_budget`. FINDINGS R43-ladder pushed. Pod reclaimed.

## Headline: the R41 Rung-1 win did NOT transfer to real text

OLD-only held-out paraphrase EM (all old items), per seed:

| arm | seed0 | seed1 | mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| k0 (no replay) | 0.217 | 0.267 | 0.242 |
| full (ceiling) | 0.483 | 0.483 | **0.483** |
| surprise@0.125 | 0.284 | 0.433 | 0.359 |
| random@0.125 | 0.300 | 0.367 | 0.334 |
| **surprise@0.25** | 0.333 | 0.533 | 0.433 |
| **random@0.25** | 0.467 | 0.450 | **0.459** |
| lowbits@0.25 | 0.350 | 0.350 | 0.350 |
| surprise@0.5 | 0.400 | 0.550 | 0.475 |
| random@0.5 | 0.450 | 0.567 | **0.509** |

**Fails your pre-registered gate** (surprise > random BOTH seeds): sign INVERTS across seeds at B=0.125
and 0.25; on the mean random ≥ surprise at B≥0.25. This is a null leaning random, high seed variance.

## But the surprise SIGNAL is real — it's the ROUTER that adds nothing

`surprise_summary` (skipped old items, base-bits vs retention): corr = −0.29 (k0) … −0.43 (@0.5);
k0 retention by bits tercile = **0.45 / 0.175 / 0.10** (low→high bpt). High-bpt old items forget ~4×
more — the signal R40/R41 found holds on real text. The problem is purely allocation:

- **No safe-to-skip class (the unimodal consequence):** surprise's SKIPPED low-bpt items decay to
  0.31–0.49 (vs R41's constructed corpus where skipped items stayed at 0.70). Real text has no "easy,
  survives-unreplayed" population because the bpt distribution is unimodal (R42/R43), not the bimodal
  squad-vs-synth split R41 exploited.
- **Replayed high-bpt items are hard to rescue** (intrinsically difficult: low RAG answerability,
  long/obscure answers). So moving budget toward them (surprise) vs random nets ~zero.

## What DID work: router-free replay subsampling

k0 0.242 → full 0.483 (replay helps a lot); **random@0.5 (0.509) ≈ full (0.483)** — half the replay
budget, RANDOMLY chosen, matches full. So the compute saving is real but it's O(½·#facts) random
subsample, NOT O(#exceptions) surprise routing. Newest not sacrificed (M_new_para ~0.42–0.50 all arms).

## My read
The census program did exactly what it was for: it caught that R41's optimistic Rung-1 result was an
artifact of the constructed bimodal 50/50 corpus and does NOT transfer to real text. The mechanism is
precisely the unimodality R42/R43 measured. This is a clean, mechanistically-explained negative that I
think we should own, not spin.

## Questions for you
1. **Do you accept this NULL, or want ≥2 more seeds first?** 2 seeds + n_old=60 = high variance
   (surprise@0.25 was 0.333 vs 0.533). I lean: the mean + the corr + the mechanism are consistent
   enough to call it "no reliable routing advantage on real text", but a 4-seed confirm is cheap (~one
   pod). Your call on whether the null needs tightening before we pivot.
2. **Given the null, is the right pivot your own brainstorm B — schema-compressibility?** i.e. are the
   high-bpt real exceptions COMPRESSIBLE (cluster by relation/predicate, train a few, test held-out
   same-schema) so cost moves O(#exceptions)→O(#patterns)? That attacks the actual structure real text
   has (unimodal but maybe schema-clustered) rather than item routing, which just failed.
3. **Or go straight to the dC/dbook 2-book cross-book probe** (the honest continual number)? The ladder
   was single-corpus; it says nothing about cross-book interference. Arguably that's more important than
   tightening a null on a mechanism we now understand.
4. **Reframe check:** is "random@0.5 ≈ full" actually the honest positive to report — that on real text
   you get a 2× replay-compute saving for free by subsampling, and the research question shifts from
   "which items" to "how few items / how to compress them"? That feels like the real finding.

Push back hard if I'm misreading the null as stronger than it is, or if 2 seeds is too thin to conclude.
