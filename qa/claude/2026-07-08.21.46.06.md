## What it does
- **Domains:** `squad_human` (SQuAD dev passages + HUMAN QAs — generation-free anchor; also
  calibrates the 3B faithfulness judge), `wiki_gen` (wikimedia/wikipedia streaming, 3 offsets
  2k/60k/200k to dodge the popular-head bias), `news_gen` (cnn_dailymail test — off-wiki domain).
- **Probe gen (3B, loaded once):** extract ≤6 atomic QA pairs/passage, category-labeled
  (entity|relation|number|date|causal|procedural|claim), TAB format. Filters: answer must be a
  verbatim span in passage (extractive precheck), 1–8 answer tokens, question must not contain the
  normalized answer, dedup within passage.
- **Quality audit:** (a) 3B yes/no entailment judge on ALL probes incl. human ones → judge
  calibration on squad_human; (b) dedup clusters = (pid, normalized answer), dup rate reported,
  density computed on gated = non-dup ∧ (faithful ∨ human); (c) confound strata: tail stats by
  ans_kind (number/date | proper | other) and answer-token-length bins (1-2 | 3-4 | 5+);
  (d) paraphrase stability: 2nd paraphrase via a DIFFERENTLY-WORDED instruction channel (greedy
  decoding would reproduce paraphrase #1 verbatim from the same prompt) on a 120-probe subset →
  mean |Δbpt| + corr; (e) 0.5B gold-passage RAG EM = harness-compat answerability (reported, not
  gated — the 0.5B is a weak reader; the 3B entailment is the faithfulness gate).
- **Scoring surface = `qa_answer_bits` from `s3/wikibridge.py`** (imported, not copied): same
  closed-book template, same answer-token masking; headline unit = base_bits_para / ans_ntok,
  identical to `_bpt` in the R41 harness.
- **Density deliverable:** per-domain tail fraction at τ∈{6,8,10} with **passage-level bootstrap
  CIs** (1000 resamples over pids), full curve τ=2..16, deciles — reported for **all gated** probes
  AND for **need_write** (base_em_para==0) probes. No binary-class pretense; anchors squad≈4.45 /
  synth≈11.37 bpt stored in config for reference.
- **Two-hop canary:** ≤20 passages/domain, one combine-two-facts question each, faithfulness-judged,
  scored, reported separately, NON-gating (your composition metadata seed).
- Outputs: `census_result.json` (config+summary+passages incl. contexts for Step-B reuse) +
  `census_result.jsonl` (per-probe rows). Inference-only — no training anywhere.

## Known compromises (flag if any is disqualifying)
1. **Judge = generator (both Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct).** Self-agreement bias: the 3B may bless its own
   generations. Mitigation: extractive-span precheck is independent, and squad_human calibrates the
   judge's rejection behavior on known-good probes. A second judge model would double pod cost for
   Step A; I think calibration + extractive constraint is enough for a density estimate. Agree?
2. **Wiki passages = article HEAD (first ~1400 chars).** Lead sections are summary-like (more
   on-manifold than deep-body text) → density may be UNDERestimated for wiki. The 3 stream offsets
   mitigate popularity bias but not lead-section bias. Accept for Step A, note in report?
3. **Gradient/write-conflict proxy: deferred** (your "optional subset-only" item) — keeps Step A
   strictly inference-only; can add on the high/low extremes in Step B's pod where a backward pass
   is already paid for.
4. **cnn_dailymail as the "book-like" stand-in.** True book chapters (Gutenberg) have flaky HF
   loaders; news is a robust off-wiki real-text domain. If you know a reliable HF book dataset with
   a stable schema, name it and I'll add it as a 4th domain.
