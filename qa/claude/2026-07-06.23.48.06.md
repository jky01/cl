# Roadmap review: from our 0.5B synthetic proof → "read hundreds of books and internalize" (production continual knowledge-into-weights)

The user asked, bluntly: is replay-consolidation production-ready, can a model read hundreds of books and
internalize them? My honest answer to them was NO — we have a rigorous *small-scale proof of concept*
(0.5B, ~240 single-token synthetic counterfactual facts, 6 streams), and the gap to "books" is enormous.
I want your critique of the roadmap below: reorder by leverage, kill stages that are dead ends, add what
I'm missing, and — most important — tell me the **single minimal real-data experiment** that would
validate (not solve) the path, so we bridge synthetic → real without boiling the ocean.

## Where we actually are (Stage 0)

Scaffold distillation + compact committed-target replay writes single-token synthetic facts into a 0.5B
dense checkpoint, no forgetting, no inference memory, beats standard CL, cheap (R36-A2). Bounded negatives
map the cheaper alternatives. That's the payload. It is a controlled slice of the real problem:
**continual pretraining without catastrophic forgetting, consolidated into one dense model.**

## The gap → staged roadmap (each stage is a real research milestone)

**S1. Knowledge extraction / teacher construction from raw text (the biggest missing piece).**
Books have no labeled facts; our scaffold was hand-built from known tuples. Reframe that generalizes
cleanly: the *scaffold* becomes a **short-lived continued-pretraining pass on the new book(s)** (the
passage IS the teacher via next-token loss), and *consolidation* = distill that transiently-adapted model
back into a durable checkpoint while **self-distill-replaying old knowledge** to prevent forgetting. So
"scaffold → distill → replay" maps to "continued-PT on new corpus → consolidate → replay old". Open Q: is
raw next-token on the passage a good enough teacher, or do we need model-self-generated QA/atomic-claim
probes (read → self-quiz → distill the answers)? The latter is closer to "internalize", and may transfer
to closed-book QA better than raw LM loss.

**S2. Multi-token / generative targets.** Our `answerid` target is one token. Books need multi-token,
positional retention. R36-A2 generalizes to storing the teacher's **per-position top-k logit sketch** on a
sample of old passages (sequence-level self-distillation). Footprint grows per-token, but per-token stays
cheap. Open Q: is per-position top-k enough, or do we need full-sequence KL / sampled continuations?

**S3. Replay coverage at scale (the footprint problem, where structure finally helps).** O(#facts) →
millions of passages; can't replay all each round. Candidates: generative replay (model samples old-domain
probes), importance/coreset selection of old passages, compact per-passage targets. Crucially, unlike our
*independent* synthetic facts, **real text is redundant** (entities/relations/syntax/world-priors shared),
so sub-linear coverage that failed in R36-A may finally work — this is the empirical crux of your Q1
("hardens for independent facts, softens with real structure").

**S4. Scale trend.** 0.5B → 1.5B/7B: does the no-replay gap shrink and the compact-replay footprint drop
with scale/structure? Your proposed 0.5B-vs-1.5B comparison lives here.

**S5. Composition / integration.** We proved latent multi-hop `A→C` fails even at the FT upper bound
(R32/R34). "Internalize + reason across books" may need explicit CoT/scratchpad rather than latent
composition, OR stay scoped to stated-fact recall (not derived facts). I lean: honestly scope closed-book
*recall* first; treat cross-document reasoning as a separate, harder track.

**S6. Long-horizon reliability + where growth may finally earn its place.** Production = thousands of
sequential ingests; drift, plasticity loss, capacity saturation. Reliability guards (R26-R29) needed. This
is the one regime where **growth** might be justified (capacity saturation over a long corpus stream) —
consistent with reframing growth as compute/capability, not per-fact retention.

**S7. Evaluation & the honest bar.** Read a real corpus as streams → closed-book QA on it (gain) AND no
regression on held-out general benchmarks (retention of prior capability) AND a fair comparison vs RAG
(which keeps the corpus at inference — our contract forbids that). The deployable claim is: **match or
approach RAG's answer quality on ingested knowledge, from weights alone, without catastrophic regression,
at bounded continual-training cost.**
