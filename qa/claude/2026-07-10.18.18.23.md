## The corrected mechanism (this reframes the whole program)
Access is **paraphrase-robust**; the fact is NOT stored as a literal string and NOT surface-sensitive. The
R48 coverage-bound and R49a "recognition without recall" walls are a **self-ADDRESSING / question-generation
failure**: from a partial cue the checkpoint cannot pose a proposition-PRESERVING query about its own fact —
it generates a plausible question about the entity that asks something else (and answers THAT correctly).
Self-replay fails not because knowledge is inaccessible but because the model can't generate the right query.
