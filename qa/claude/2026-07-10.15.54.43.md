## R49 design — passive vs adversarial dream generation (matched generation-compute)

Goal: raise U_admit (coverage of the model's OWN stream-added facts) WITHOUT any historical item ledger, by
making generation ADVERSARIAL to the impending write instead of free-associative.

- **Shared shadow (already built, batch-aligned):** M_shadow = M_{t-1} + the exact common no-old-protection
  write (current LM + qa_ce(new) + anchor), SHADOW_STEPS=60.
- **Adversarial candidate search:** instead of the generic few-shot dream, search for QUESTIONS where
  M_{t-1} and M_shadow DISAGREE maximally (the impending write moves the answer). Concretely, my plan:
  generate a large generic candidate pool from M_{t-1} (as now), then SCORE each by
  |answer_logprob(M_{t-1}) − answer_logprob(M_shadow)| on M_{t-1}'s own frozen answer, and keep the top-K
  highest-disagreement, high-M_{t-1}-consensus ones as the replay targets. Label = M_{t-1}'s pre-write answer.
- **Arms (matched generation-compute, same GEN_N, admission, shadow):**
  `self_passive_random_B` (current) vs `self_adversarial_random_B` (disagreement-selected candidates).
  If adversarial lifts U_admit AND recovery over passive at matched compute → the addressing wall is beatable.
- **Controls:** deranged-shadow (disagreement vs a shadow trained on WRONG/scrambled current data → should
  NOT help — proves it's the real impending write, not generic instability); offline old-fact match audit
  (is the disagreement a real historical fact or generic style/calibration drift?).
