## Three conclusions

1. **The v1 fold wall was an artifact of exact-logit-MSE preservation, exactly as you predicted.**
   Your compatibility control is the smoking gun: the joint witness perfectly classifies A∪B yet its
   A-logits sit **MSE 303.6** away from the A-only teacher. So "fit A∪B labels" and "match A's exact
   logits" are almost orthogonal — the exact-logit target is off the manifold of B-compatible
   solutions. As alpha rises the mse fold drives A_logMSE down (20.9→0.5) and that is *precisely* what
   kills B. Confirmed: the penalty, not the capacity or the path.

2. **Decision-preserving preservation (CE on A's own argmax pseudo-labels) folds cleanly to 1.0/1.0,
   slots deleted, at every alpha.** It *lets* A-logits drift hugely (A_logMSE 269–364) while holding
   accA=1.0 with healthy margin (6.8–9.3, well above the pre-fold 10.3, so not a margin-collapse fake).
   Init-independent (A-init and fresh-init both succeed) → not path dependence; the trust region just
   has to constrain **decisions, not activations**.

3. So at a width where a union solution exists, **consolidation into fixed shared weights is real**:
   a *label-free-for-old-data* self-distillation (CE on A's own predictions) + B labels puts both into
   the shared checkpoint with no inference-time slots. This is a genuine knowledge-into-weights fold.

## The honest boundary, and the next round

The CE fold still uses **all of A's inputs** as the preservation support — your full-A oracle referee.
It proves the *mechanism exists*; it is not yet deployable. Note the distinction it already buys: CE
uses A's **inputs** but not A's **gold labels** (self-distillation on argmax), so it's label-free for
old data — but still input-replay. The real continual-learning difficulty lives in reducing the
referee, so next round I'll sweep it:

- **A-input referee budget:** full A → random bounded subsets (e.g. 50%, 10%, 2%) → zero, all via CE
  self-distillation on the retained inputs only. Measure accA/accB/min + A-margin as the budget falls.
  Prediction: retention degrades as the referee shrinks; the curve locates the deployable gap.
- **Input-free variant** (the honest hard case): no A inputs retained at all — can anything stand in?
  In this discriminative key→class toy there's no legal way to regenerate A's *keys* (you flagged
  this), so a zero-input referee here means "fold B with no A support" = naive forgetting. That's the
  floor. This is exactly why the toy can't answer the self-generated-replay question and the LM/SQuAD
  harness has to — an LM *can* re-derive old inputs from its own distribution; a random-key classifier
  cannot.
- **Parameter-matched growth control** at a width where even bounded-referee CE fails: does adding
  shared width (vs private slots) let a smaller referee suffice? That's where growth would earn its
  place.
