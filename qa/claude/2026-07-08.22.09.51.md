## Findings

1. **Real tail at the off-manifold anchor (τ=10) is 7–14%; at τ=8, 21–29%.** The constructed 50/50
   overstated real tails ~4–7×. B≈0.2–0.3 surprise-routed budget should suffice on real text →
   O(#exceptions) is a real compute advantage. **Rung-2 density trigger NOT met** (CI-upper 0.20–0.37
   at τ=8 vs your ≈0.5 trigger).
2. **The distribution is smooth and UNIMODAL** (squad deciles 2.56→9.67, no gap) — exactly your
   anticipated case: no natural binary exception class in real text. τ is a budget/risk knob.
3. **Type structure:** number/date answers ~never in the τ=8 tail (0–2%); proper names 12–43%;
   open-class phrases 38–50%. Novel NAMES are the real-text analogue of R40's invented-name synth.
4. **Length confound (your requested table, and it BITES):** 1–2-token answers tail@8 = 0.43–0.68 vs
   5+ tokens 0.05–0.19. Later answer tokens condition on earlier ones → per-token surprise amortizes.
   So raw bpt over-selects short answers.
5. **Deictic-probe contamination (my spot-check):** part of the news tail is context-dependent
   probes — "What is the name of the dog?"→Sam, "When did the LHC start circulating?"→Sunday. These
   are not globally addressable facts; closed-book they are unanswerable in principle. The news 14%
   is an UPPER bound on knowledge-novelty density.
6. Two-hop canary: 33 faithful, mean 5.71 ≈ one-hop distribution — composition is not
   surprise-visible at the span level (consistent with R32/R34: a different axis than storage cost).

## Step B design questions (before I build the real-stratified ladder)

**Q1 — length confound handling in `select_budget`.** Options: (a) keep raw bpt (matches R41,
simple, but over-selects short answers); (b) select within answer-length strata (1-2/3-4/5+),
budget split proportionally; (c) regress bpt on ans_ntok over the census and use the residual as
the router. I lean (b): stratified selection keeps the router training-free and kills the confound
mechanically; (c) adds a fitted component I'd rather not introduce yet; (a) risks the ladder
measuring "short-answer replay" instead of "exception replay". Your call.

**Q2 — self-containedness screen.** Add a 3B judge pass at stream-build time: "Could someone who
knows the relevant facts answer this question without the passage? yes/no" and drop noes. Kills
deictic probes (finding 5) before they enter the ladder as fake exceptions. Cheap (one more judge
call per probe). Any objection?

**Q3 — grid anchoring.** Your formula {f/2, f, 2f, 0.5}: which f? Candidates: f=0.25 (τ=8 pooled
across domains, mid-threshold) → grid {0.125, 0.25, 0.5} (2f=0.5 collapses into the sign-stability
point — convenient, 3 budget points); or f=0.10 (τ=10, anchor-calibrated) → {0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5}.
I lean f=0.25/τ=8: the unimodal shape means τ=10 is deep-tail and B=0.05 pools get tiny (n≈6 of 60
old items per seed — no statistical power at our scale). At our n, {0.125, 0.25, 0.5} × SEEDS=2 with
all 6 arms at 0.5 is the powered version of your tail-aware grid.

**Q4 — stream composition for Step B.** Natural-density view: build streams from census
passages/probes as-is (squad_human + wiki_gen + news_gen mixed, their own density). Balanced audit
view: oversample census high-bpt + low-bpt probes for router power. You proposed both if budget
allows; with ~450 gated probes we can fund natural-density (3 streams × ~3 articles × ~5 probes ≈ 45
committed/seed... thin). Alternative: natural-density PRIMARY at Step-B scale (raise CN passages in
a rerun of the census to ~triple probes — it's 3 min of GPU), balanced audit view SECONDARY only at
B=0.25. How would you allocate?
