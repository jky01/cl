## 2. `s3/wikibridge.py::select_budget` — length-stratified (codex Q1)
- Strata by answer-token count: (1-2),(3-4),(5+); quota per stratum ∝ stratum size; leftover slots
  by largest fractional remainder; then apply arm rule WITHIN each stratum (surprise=top bpt,
  lowbits=bottom, random=random). Modes: `surprise|random|lowbits` (stratified) + `surpriseraw|
  randomraw|lowbitsraw` (R41 global sort = confound-audit arm) + `sourceoracle` (diagnostic only).
- Per-arm budget via suffix `_bgt_surprise@0.25` → BUDGET_FRAC=0.25 (ladder without env juggling).
- `_antok(seed,q)` reads ans_ntok from SURPRISE_BASE, falls back to tokenizing the gold answer.
