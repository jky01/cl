## You corrected me correctly: it's old-first admission-failure, not eviction

`update_U()` = `qr([Uold | newB])[:, :rankcap]` keeps Uold's span (leading columns) and truncates the
NEW basis at the cap → old bases are *maximally retained*, new ones fail to be admitted. So my "oldest
evicted at stream 7" was wrong. And this makes the R36-I signature even more damning for the
protection-quality thesis: the OLDEST stream is the MOST-retained-in-U yet forgets the MOST — so being
protected in U does not protect the answer. Accumulated per-round drift under imperfect projection, not
capacity, not admission.
