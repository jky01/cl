# Built R51 Phase A horizon ladder (fixed-small frontier: capacity vs dilution). Review while it runs.

Source: `qa/codex/2026-07-10.22.14.28.md`. `s2/horizon_ladder.py` (191 lines, compiles). Note: the census
artifact actually has 67 usable passages (460 gated probes), so STREAMS=9 (H/3H/9H via the retention-vs-horizon
curve) IS feasible on existing data — I'm running the pilot now; corpus expansion to "hundreds of books" is a
follow-up if this shows a wall worth chasing.

One census lineage per arm over STREAMS=9; measures OLD-only held-out paraphrase EM vs horizon (full curve, not
just 3 points). Arms share the per-seed manifest, differ only in retention mechanism:
- `fixed_1x`: budget replay (0.5*committed random), 1x cons — the baseline that may dilute.
- `fixed_4x`: 4x consolidation steps — does more compute restore?
- `full_cue`: replay from ALL committed — does full coverage restore?
- `const_expo`: EXPOSURE=6 replay draws PER committed item / stream (extra O(history) replay-only passes) — the
  decisive dilution control. If it restores retention, the wall is dilution, NOT capacity.
- `no_cue`: no old replay (compute-matched filler) — floor.
`decide()`: fixed_1x declines ≥0.10 both seeds AND none of const_expo/full/4x restore → `capacity_wall_candidate`
(N* → Phase B growth); const_expo/full/4x restores → `rehearsal_dilution_wall` (growth NOT justified); no decline
→ `no_saturation_in_range`. Logs old-EM by age, accumulated frozen-base surprise bits, cue bytes, draws/item.

Three checks I want before I trust the verdict:
1. **Is `const_expo` the right dilution isolator?** I give each committed item ~EXPOSURE=6 draws/stream via extra
   replay-only passes (O(committed) compute). But this also adds raw optimizer steps vs fixed_1x — so a "restore"
   could be extra COMPUTE not constant EXPOSURE per se. Should I compute-match const_expo against fixed_Nx at the
   same total steps, or is the point precisely that constant exposure COSTS O(history) compute (and fixed_4x is
   the compute-matched-but-still-diluting control that separates the two)? I think fixed_4x vs const_expo IS the
   clean contrast (same-ish compute, different allocation), but want your ruling.
2. **Acquisition is common but replay ADDS a qa_ce block** — so replay arms do more compute/step than no_cue.
   no_cue gets a filler qa_ce(new) block to match. Is per-step block-count matching enough, or do you want
   explicit token/FLOP accounting to call it compute-matched?
3. **N* by accumulated surprise vs stream index**: I checkpoint retention at every stream t and report surprise
   bits, but the "horizon" x-axis in decide() uses first-vs-last committed-old EM. Should the decline test be on
   the surprise-bit axis (interpolate to fixed surprise levels) rather than first/last stream, so seeds with
   different commit rates are comparable?
