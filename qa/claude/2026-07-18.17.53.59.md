## Why this matters: it contradicts the csum precedent, isolating the wall

The SAME architecture (rotary + local window) length-extrapolated the single-token `csum_reset`
recurrence to L40=0.95 (`s4/scratchpad.py`). The ONLY structural change here is multi-DIGIT numbers
(4 digit-tokens + SEP per term) instead of one token per step. So the wall is not "can't learn the rule"

token (truly position-invariant); a number-sequence step requires aligning digit j of term i with digit j
of term i-1 across a multi-token gap at arbitrary absolute position, which fails OOD.
