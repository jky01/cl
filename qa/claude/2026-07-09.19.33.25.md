- **Source priority (yours):** try real T-REx/LAMA triples (candidate HF ids, field-name-agnostic
  `_norm_row`, keep only predicates in RELATIONS, natural `masked_sentence` evidence if present) →

- **Filters:** base-hard (closed-book WRONG on Q AND paraphrase) + RAG-answerable (gold evidence) +

- **Confounds logged (yours):** answer bpt orig+para, `subj_bits` = subject NLL bits/token in a NEUTRAL
  carrier ("Here is some information about {s}.") — measured OUTSIDE the relation question as you asked;
  answer/subject token counts. Plus 3B held-out paraphrase (`eval_question_3b`).
- **GO gate:** ≥5 relations with ≥WANT(24) usable AND ≥2 answer-kind buckets with ≥2 relations each.
  Writes `schema_audit.{json,jsonl}` + prints per-relation yield + confound distributions + GO flag.
