# Open strategic question (project lead → us): are we actually walking toward the ultimate goal?

This is NOT a narrow experiment question. The project lead asked, in effect: *given everything so far,
do you believe this path reaches the ultimate goal, and can we keep going?* I gave my honest read below;
I want your genuine, adversarial assessment — agree, disagree, or reframe. Do not be diplomatic. If you
think I'm being too pessimistic OR too optimistic, say which and why, grounded in the repo's evidence
(`FINDINGS.md`, `docs/RESEARCH_MAP.md`, `docs/cloud_results/`).

## The ultimate goal (restated)
A small model continually READS (hundreds of books), internalizes new knowledge INTO WEIGHTS, GROWS
into a larger model, with no catastrophic forgetting, no full joint retraining, no external memory at
inference. Continual learning as an unsolved frontier.

## My honest scorecard

**Solid positives (real):** knowledge-into-weights via replay/self-distill beats CL baselines (R33
all-seen 0.914, no inference memory); real-text bridge ~92% of RAG (R38); replay works (k0 0.24 → full
0.48); **random@0.5 ≈ full** (router-free 2× replay saving, R43); surprise is a genuine RISK signal
(high-bpt forgets ~4×, corr −0.3); real exception density measured (unimodal, τ8 16–25%, τ10 6–8%).

**Solid negatives (also real):** growth NOT justified at this scale (R23/R31/R32/R34); composition/
abstraction does NOT emerge (R32/R34); surprise-as-ROUTER fails on real text (R43-ladder, today);
keytie/merge/EWC retired; nswrite saturates.

## My three-walls claim
The ultimate goal is blocked by three walls, and we've now hit all three:
1. **Rehearsal-free CL (interference):** our best is still rehearsal. Every rehearsal-free mechanism
   we tried either doesn't transfer or is negative.
2. **Composition/abstraction:** zero positive signal. Deepest wall. "Read a book" ≠ "memorize QAs".
3. **Growth necessity/mechanism:** we can't even measure WHEN to grow.

## My judgment (the part I most want you to attack)
- The **engineering sub-goal** (facts into weights, no inference memory, compact-replay protection) is
  essentially DONE and solid — but it costs O(fraction·#facts) rehearsal, which is the baseline, not the
  breakthrough.
- The **frontier sub-goal** (rehearsal-free + compositional + growth-justified + sublinear) is unsolved
  by us AND by the field. Frontier labs handle new knowledge via retrieval/long-context (we rejected,
  KV-cache cost), periodic full retrain (rejected), or heavy-replay finetune (= what we have, at cost).
- My probability estimate: with the current mechanism family (replay + routing + local write), reaching
  the FULL goal is **low**. We keep circling the same obstacle and every tool tops out in the same place.
- BUT continuing to (a) bank the engineering win and (b) take real frontier shots + (c) map precisely
  what fails and why — is worthwhile and viable.

## The three ambition levels I offered the lead
1. Bank the engineering result + take 2–3 real frontier shots (accepting high failure odds).
2. Reframe the goal to "achievable + valuable": an honest knowledge-into-weights + compact-replay system
   + a precise frontier map. We're nearly there.
3. Bet on a mechanism-level pivot: admit the replay family is topped out, try genuinely-untried things
   (test-time-training / state-as-weights, predictive coding, surprise as a COMPRESSION-opportunity
   detector not a router). High risk / high reward.

## What I want from you
1. **Is my "low probability for the full goal on this mechanism family" honest, or am I giving up too
   early?** Point to specific repo evidence that either supports the ceiling claim or shows an
   unexploited direction I'm undervaluing.
2. **Is the composition wall (R32/R34) actually fatal, or is it an artifact of synthetic data / 0.5B
   scale / our specific eval?** This is the crux — if composition is reachable on REAL text with a
   pretrained base's existing schemas, the whole picture changes. Give me your real prior.
3. **If you had to pick ONE shot most likely to move a wall** (not polish), which of: schema-
   compressibility, a genuinely rehearsal-free interference mechanism, state-as-weights/TTT, or a
   compression-detector reframe — and WHY, with a falsifiable 1-pod test?
4. **Is there a reframing of the ultimate goal itself** that is both honest AND still ambitious — i.e.
   not surrender, but a version that's actually reachable and that a small-model-grows story can truly
   claim? (E.g. "continual SKILL/schema acquisition with bounded forgetting" vs "unbounded fact
   memorization".)

Be blunt. If the honest answer is "the full goal is not reachable here and the valuable move is to
reframe", say that plainly. If you think there's a real shot we're underrating, name it and stake out why.
