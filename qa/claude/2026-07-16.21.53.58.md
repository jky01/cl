It fits `accA_all ≈ budget·1.0 + (1-budget)·0.088` to three decimals: retained-A is perfectly kept,

indistinguishable from random**. Exactly your designed impossibility control: independent random
facts have no recoverable structure, so no finite support constrains the unretained points and no
selection rule can help. The toy has now (a) located the replay-support law (linear, slope 1 on
incompressible facts), (b) falsified the MSE invariant, (c) established the invariant-choice finding.

## The decisive next experiment: a compressible task

The whole leverage lives in compressibility, so I'm building a structured toy where facts SHARE
generative structure, then re-running the exact same fold + referee sweep. Design I'm going with
(tell me if you'd change it):

- **Facts as (entity, relation) → object**, with `n_ent` entities × `n_rel` relations. The object is a
  deterministic function of LOW-RANK entity and relation codes: e.g. `obj = g(W_r · z_e)` for latent
  entity vectors `z_e` (dim k ≪ n_ent) and per-relation maps — so the full table is generated by a
  small rule and is genuinely compressible. Input to the model is (entity_id, relation_id) via frozen
  random embeddings (still forcing knowledge into shared weights, as before).
- Task A / task B split by a held-out set of (entity,relation) pairs such that B contains pairs whose
  entities/relations also appear in A (shared structure) — so consolidating A should transfer to B and
  a small A-referee should constrain many A facts.
- Predictions to test: (i) joint reaches 1.0 at far smaller h than the random task (compressibility);
  (ii) the CE fold's retention-vs-referee curve is **sub-linear** (a small support pins many facts);
  (iii) low-margin and interference-oracle selection **beat** stratified random; (iv) generalization
  to unretained-A is > 0, unlike the random task.

Two design checks for you:

1. Is `(entity,relation)→object` via low-rank codes the right minimal compressible structure, or would
   you prefer something with explicit **compositional** generalization (e.g. object = f(entity) ∘
   h(relation) so novel unseen pairs are inferable)? Compositionality is closer to real facts but adds
   a systematic-generalization confound; I lean to start with low-rank-shared (retention/compression)
   before compositional (inference of unseen pairs).
2. For the interference-oracle selection (your #3, the illegal diagnostic): rank A pairs by margin drop
   after a short provisional B update. Agreed it's an oracle and only tells us what a legal detector
   must approximate — I'll label it as such and keep stratified-random + low-margin as the legal pair.
