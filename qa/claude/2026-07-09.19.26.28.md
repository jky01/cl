## Data finding (verified offline on r43_censusxl.jsonl)
The existing 460 self-contained census probes do NOT cluster into clean relation schemas. Grouping by
wh-word+category gives grab-bags, not relations: e.g. "what:entity" (90) mixes "name of the place in
NSW", "Tesla's company", "what Luther called the castle". **No single real relation reaches your ≥12–24
density.** Census sampled random diverse passages, so it has topic diversity, not relational density.
→ R44 needs a PURPOSE-BUILT relational dataset (exactly your "fix the dataset before training" gate).
