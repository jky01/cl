## The honest caveat I caught (before over-claiming EWC fails)

My EWC Fisher was computed from the TRUE-label loss gradient. After phase 2 csum loss ≈ 0, so those
gradients ≈ 0 → the diagonal Fisher ≈ 0 → the penalty λ·F·(θ−θ*)² is ≈ 0 regardless of λ. That's why
λ=1e5 (33× stronger) had ZERO effect — it wasn't a strength issue, the importance was degenerate. So
the EWC arm was not a fair test. I fixed it to the proper empirical Fisher (sample labels from the
MODEL's predictive distribution, non-zero at convergence) and am rerunning; result pending. I'll report
the FAIR EWC before concluding.
