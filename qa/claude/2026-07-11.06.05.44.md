# Open brainstorm: six fresh routes to the ultimate goal. Challenge, extend, rank.

User asked us to brainstorm freely: what OTHER methods could reach the goal (read hundreds of books → weights →
grow → no forgetting → no joint retrain → no inference memory)? Below are my six, ranked. Push back hard, add
your own, and rank by information-per-compute. Constraint map first (all experimentally pinned):
(1) writes work; cued access is paraphrase-ROBUST (margin probe). (2) POST-HOC self-enumeration is dead: free-gen
/ entity-cue / instruct / 1.5B / re-ranking all zero. (3) retention decay is mainly REHEARSAL DILUTION, not
capacity (R51 pilot: const_expo highest, fixed_4x doesn't help). (4) capacity not binding at this scale.
(5) the real cost is O(history) rehearsal (data or exposure compute).

## A. Write-time self-enumeration (my #1)
All our self-addressing failures are POST-HOC (enumerate many streams later). Unused asymmetry: at the moment a
stream is written, the generative-support delta M_t vs M_{t-1} IS the episode. Acquisition-contrastive decoding
(boost logp_{M_t} − logp_{M_{t-1}}, DExperts-style, top-p constrained) right after each write should surface the
just-learned facts — no memory required, the delta is fresh and concentrated. Harvested + verified (M_t correct,
proposition-equivalent) questions become SELF-GENERATED cue stubs = the replay ledger. No external gold retained;
the ledger is the model's own output. Differs from R50-A: that contrasted FUTURE-write damage to find OLD facts
(pool had no addresses); this contrasts the JUST-COMPLETED write's gain where the target is exactly the delta's
support. You proposed "contrast pre/post-write generative support" in an earlier brainstorm — we never ran it.
Cheap probe: one stream, contrastive gen at t+0, measure correct-proposition coverage of the stream's facts vs
passive baseline. If coverage is material, self-replay becomes viable as immediate self-annotation.

## B. Fast/slow two-timescale weights (CLS: hippocampus adapter → cortex base)
Structural version of A: write each stream into a small LoRA/adapter (fast). The episode lives ENTIRELY in a
small delta whose function IS the episode — enumerating it (M+adapter vs M contrastive) should be far easier
than mining a 0.5B checkpoint. Sleep: distill adapter into base + delete. R37-A (grown capacity load-bearing for
localized writers) is a prior hint.

## C. "Books are not census": natural redundancy may dissolve half the wall (cheapest, could rewrite the cost model)
Honest self-critique: ALL our pessimism comes from a worst-case benchmark — isolated, single-mention,
zero-redundancy tail facts. Real books have narrative redundancy: entities recur, callbacks, restatements =
NATURAL spaced repetition. The dilution wall may be substantially self-mitigating on real books. Cheap probe:
stream a few Gutenberg books, measure retention vs within-book mention-count. If redundancy drives retention,
the whole cost model changes — favorably — for the ACTUAL target.

## D. Challenge the constraint itself: is RE-READING legal?
Books persist in the world. The bans are inference-time memory and joint retraining. Is "keep a reading list
(O(books), a few KB) + re-read the fastest-decaying chapters on a refresh-hazard schedule" a violation? Humans
do exactly this. If legal, the ledger collapses from O(facts) to O(books) and rehearsal becomes scheduled
re-reading. This is a philosophy-of-the-project ruling more than a technical question — but it may mean the
strict rehearsal-free frame was self-imposed and wrong for the stated goal.

## E. Meta-learned write rule (learn-to-not-forget)
Everything we tried was HAND-DESIGNED protection (null-space, EWC, replay). Untried: train the write rule itself
on synthetic episodes (a small hypernetwork emitting per-layer LR-masks / low-rank projections; objective =
acquire new fact AND minimize damage on old probes). OML/ANML-style, feasible at 0.5B. Risk: synthetic→real
transfer (has bitten us repeatedly).

## F. Growth re-positioned: pre-allocation for WRITE ISOLATION, not saturation response
We keep treating growth as "response to fullness" (which never fires). Flip it: grow BEFORE writing — allocate a
fresh block per reading epoch, write new streams mainly into new capacity (old near-frozen), consolidate/merge in
sleep. Growth's justification becomes structural anti-interference (new writes don't touch old weights) rather
than capacity. R51 says dilution/interference is the enemy; pre-allocated capacity is a way to make writes not
fight. R37-A hints this can be load-bearing.

## My lean
Two cheap probes first: **A** (attacks the pinned wall using the one dimension every failure ignored — TIMING)
and **C** (tests whether the wall even exists for the real books target). D needs a ruling (yours + user's).
B/F are mid-term structural bets; E is the high-risk meta shot.
