## Adopted without changes

  M_{t-1} generates a fixed N candidate QA under fixed generic instructions; gold match is OFFLINE AUDIT
  ONLY, never influences generation/filtering/selection. `indexed_self_replay` (subject-keyed) only ships
  under its own name with its O(facts) scaling curve — not as the ledger-free arm.
- **Two-stage separation of reliability vs fragility.** Stage-1 admit by cross-paraphrase consensus +
  answer-margin floor -> freeze pre-update canonical CE target. Stage-2 fragility = shadow-update
  susceptibility (answer-margin drop / flip after a brief current-stream shadow update), NOT low
  consistency. self_random_B and self_fragile_B draw from the SAME admitted pool + SAME canonical targets.
- **S_t is not an oracle** (inherits M_{t-1}'s old-fact errors, no new evidence). Dropped.
- **successor_readout deferred** to the synthetic R47 suite (real-text version is latent replay: storing
  the endpoint center is rehearsal regardless of the "synthetic perturbation" wrapper). Agreed.
- **4 arms**: no_replay_compute_matched / stored_random_B / self_random_B / self_fragile_B, matched B +
  consolidation + optimizer steps. CF smoke = mechanical shakedown only (NOT a scientific verdict); census

  +>=0.05, zero persistent old items, per-age retention (mean gain by abandoning oldest facts != pass),
  generation FLOPs reported separately.

## The lock-in push: your own design implies coverage is the binding constraint, not selection

self_fragile_B can only re-protect facts that appear in M_{t-1}'s spontaneously-generated candidate pool.
A fragile old fact the checkpoint never dreams is unreachable by ANY selector. So the fragility axis is
bounded above by generation coverage of the old, continually-added facts — and R38B/R43 already told us
item replay only protects what it touches. Therefore:

1. **The real headline is your `generated-vs-never-generated` old-EM split, not the fragility delta.** If
   self-generation never revisits the stream-added facts (dreams easy pretrained facts instead), self_random
   and self_fragile both fail for the SAME reason and the selection question is moot. I want coverage-of-
   stream-facts promoted from "audit metric" to a PRIMARY reported axis, with a preregistered floor: unique
   matched stream-facts among the N candidates must be >= some fraction of the committed stream-fact count,
   or the arc is coverage-bound and no selection result is interpretable.

2. **This makes your brainstorm #1 (adversarial dream replay) the actual mechanism, not a side idea.**
   Passive "list what you remember" generation will over-sample high-prior pretrained facts. The causal
   generator is: run a disposable shadow current-stream update, search for prompts where M_{t-1} and the
   post-update shadow DISAGREE MAXIMALLY, label with M_{t-1} pre-update, keep only high-consensus
   disagreements. That directly targets THREATENED stream-fact basins instead of easy dreams — it attacks
   coverage AND fragility at once. Controls: offline old-fact match (is the disagreement a real historical
   fact or generic style/calibration drift?) + deranged-prompt control.

   My proposal: make the generator itself an experimental axis alongside the 4 arms —
   `self_passive` (list-remembered) vs `self_adversarial` (shadow-disagreement search). If adversarial lifts
   stream-fact coverage AND recovery over passive, that's the finding; if passive already covers enough,
   we've learned self-generation is easier than feared. Either way coverage is measured, not assumed.

2. Is `self_adversarial` worth adding as a 5th arm on the FIRST census run, or do we first prove `self_passive`
