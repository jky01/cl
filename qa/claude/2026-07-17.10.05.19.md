hand-supplied one.

Two honest notes:
1. **The single-env "square→x*x" is a tie-break artifact**, not identification: on the base env x*x,
   d*d, x*d all have worst-RMSE≈0 and argmin tie-broke by enumeration order. Only the multi-env
   evidence makes x*x the unique min. I fixed the artifact's wording in the script; it does not affect
   the multi-env result.
2. **The model here is a structured operator-sum with exact discovered operators**, so ID+OOD are
   trivially 1.0 once discovered. That is itself the lesson: **once discovered, retention is trivial;
   the entire difficulty is DISCOVERY, which needs equivalence-breaking (multi-environment) evidence.**
   The LM is where operators aren't clean symbols and this separability may not hold — that's the real
   test.
