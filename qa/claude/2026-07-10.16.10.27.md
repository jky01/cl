## What it does
Per seed (census): bare-acquire streams 0..final_t-1 -> `M_prev` (knows the old facts, NO replay). Old
universe = facts from streams < final_t. Then:
- **availability**: `avail_em` = M_prev held-out-paraphrase EM; `avail_bits` = per-tok gold-answer bits.
- **threat**: `M_real` = M_prev + full 400-step bare write on the final stream; `M_shadow` = +60-step SAME
  write (same seed => batch-aligned prefix). `damage_real`/`damage_shadow` = bits rise. Threatened =
  answerable AND damage_real >= 0.5 bits/tok.
- **shadow gate**: `spearman(damage_shadow, damage_real)` (your >=0.30 requirement, reported per seed).
- **cue ladder** (generation from the SAME M_prev, matched by the R48-validated signature matcher):
  - `L0_free` = R48 generic few-shot; `L1_fixed_family` = 6 FROZEN broad-form prompts (global pool GEN_N);
  - `L2_oracle_domain` = census `src` domain word only; `L3_oracle_topic` = answer-redacted content words
    of the gold question (O(facts), audit-only); `L5_full_question` = ask M_prev the held-out paraphrase
    (answerability ceiling).
  - Records `cov_answerable`, `cov_threatened`, `correct_answerable` per level.
- **decide()** applies your frozen rules on cov_threatened (both-seed mean): availability_fail (L5<0.2) /
  fixed_family_win (L1-L0>=0.20) / search_warranted (L0,L1<0.05 & max(L2,L3)>=0.5*L5) / narrow_basin_fail
  (L2,L3<0.5*L5) / inconclusive.

## Where I want your review (I expect issues)
1. **Threatened-subset size / power.** With STREAMS=4, ARTS=5, QA=5, the old universe is ~55-60 facts, of
   which answerable maybe ~20-40, of which threatened (damage_real>=0.5 bits/tok) could be quite small
   (R48: no_replay retention ~0.38, so ~60% "forgotten" but bits-threshold threatened may differ). If
   threatened n < ~10 the cov_threatened rungs are too noisy for the +0.20 / half-ceiling gates. Should I
   (a) widen to STREAMS=6 for a bigger old universe, (b) lower DAMAGE_MIN, or (c) report cov over the
   ANSWERABLE universe as primary and threatened as secondary? I lean (c)+(a): answerable is the more
   stable denominator and the mechanism question ("can it surface its own facts") holds on answerable; the
   threatened cut is the retention-relevance filter but is where power dies first.
2. **L5 == available_em by construction.** L5 asks M_prev the held-out paraphrase, so L5 cov == avail_em
   rate. That's the ceiling by definition. Is that the right ceiling, or do you want L5 to be an
   answer-redacted ALMOST-complete question (so L5 < 1.0 leaves headroom and the "narrow_basin" vs
   "search_warranted" boundary is meaningful)? My current L5 conflates "answerable" with "ceiling."
3. **Matcher recall on generated Q.** Same Jaccard>=0.34 signature matcher R48 validated for U_admit=0. For
   the ladder, a MISS understates coverage. On oracle rungs (L2/L3) the generated Q should share more
   content words with the gold Q, so matches should be easier — but is Jaccard>=0.34 too strict when the
   cue itself seeds overlap (inflating L3 coverage artificially since the topic words ARE the gold Q's
   words)? That's a real confound: L3 coverage could be trivially high because I prompt WITH the gold Q's
   content words and then match ON content-word overlap. How do I de-confound — match L3 on ANSWER-bearing
   proposition rather than question-signature, or require the generated Q to add the relation/answer the
   cue omitted?
4. **Compute accounting.** L0/L1/L2 = one global pool (GEN_N). L3 = GEN_PER per fact (O(facts)). I report
   n_cands per level but not FLOPs. Is per-level n_cands + the O(facts) label enough for a diagnostic, or do
   you want model-token-eval counts?
5. **Is bare-acquire-then-single-transition the right rig,** or do you want the ladder measured at MULTIPLE
   transitions (each t) to see if addressability decays with age? One transition (final) is cheapest and has
   the largest old universe (your shakedown suggestion), but age-of-fact vs surfacing is exactly the kind of
   thing that might show structure.
