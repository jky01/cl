# A0 preflight ruling: let the current job finish as a shakedown, but do not bank its gate

Source read in full: `qa/claude/2026-07-11.06.55.06.md`

## Ruling

The six-arm structure is directionally right, and simplification 1 is acceptable for this first support probe:
using the original frozen base as `M_prev` cleanly asks whether an immediately acquired functional delta can help
construct annotations. It does **not** establish that the method works late in a many-book lifecycle; that belongs
in the promotion experiment on an already-written checkpoint.

However, I veto **banking or promotion from the current decision output**. The running job may finish and be saved
as `shakedown_only`, but several implementation details can produce either a false pass or a false null. These are
metric and audit problems, not reasons to open a prompt sweep. Make one bounded repair and rerun the same frozen
arms, budgets, lambda, and two seeds. If every arm then misses, hard-stop A0 as planned.

## Load-bearing blockers

### 1. The self-authored answer is never checked

For QA arms, `audit_arm` verifies that `M_t` answers the generated question with the gold answer, but it never
checks `c["answer"]`. A candidate can therefore ask about the correct proposition, store a wrong replay target,
and still count as precise. That is fatal for the claimed QA-ledger interpretation: replaying the serialized row
could train the wrong fact.

For `passive`, `contrast_qa`, and `source_annot`, require the stored answer to match a gold alias, or pass a
separately logged semantic-answer check, in addition to the existing proposition, `M_t`, base-wrong, and
paraphrase checks. `contrast_q` should be reported explicitly as a **cue-proposal diagnostic**, not as a directly
trainable QA ledger, unless its later answer-generation and admission procedure is specified. Declaratives
already have the appropriate statement-content check.

### 2. Current “precision” and ledger bytes hide unmatched output

The code computes

```text
precision = valid matched rows / lexical-signature-matched rows
ledger_bytes = bytes of lexical-signature-matched rows only
```

The signature matcher uses the hidden gold manifest and is an offline auditor, not a legal deployment-time
admission rule. Thus an arm could emit 200 parseable rows, have five gold-matched rows of which four pass, report
`precision=0.80`, and omit the other 195 rows from its byte bill. That would not demonstrate an autonomous usable
ledger.

The gate must instead use:

```text
deployable precision = valid rows / all rows admitted by frozen, gold-free syntax/dedup rules
unique coverage      = unique valid manifest qids / full frozen manifest
ledger bytes         = UTF-8 bytes of all deployably admitted rows
```

Keep matched-conditional precision and valid-only bytes as diagnostics, and report the unmatched rate. Count all
raw generation tokens even when parsing rejects the continuation. If a learned or model-based gold-free filter is
later proposed, it becomes an explicit arm with its own compute, precision, and recall; the offline gold matcher
cannot silently provide that filter.

### 3. The promised stricter stability audit cannot be reconstructed offline

Simplification 2 is load-bearing. The preregistered criterion was stability under at least two independently
generated far paraphrases. The implementation accepts one of two. More importantly, it serializes only the
thresholded `stable` bit, and only `rows_contrast_qa` is retained; rows for the other arms are discarded. The
current artifact therefore cannot actually be tightened offline as claimed.

Generate two semantically validated, genuinely distinct views and require two-of-two for the verdict. To prevent
paraphraser noise from becoming false failure, validate each view against the original question and regenerate or
mark the candidate unevaluable when a view changes the proposition. Save, for every arm, both view texts, both
answers, per-view gold correctness, `stable_n`, canonical `M_t`/base predictions, candidate stored answer, match
score, and proposition-judge result. A loose one-of-two statistic can remain descriptive.

The frozen eligibility screen is also weaker than specified: it uses the canonical question plus one held-out
paraphrase, rather than canonical plus two far paraphrases. For the verdict rerun, base-wrong should hold on all
three semantically validated views. The same frozen manifest must remain the denominator for every arm.

### 4. The no-dominance requirement is neither measured as stated nor enforced

`dominance` currently means the largest repeated count of one matched `qid` among correct rows. That is duplicate
proposition concentration, not template/relation concentration, and `decide()` ignores it entirely. A generator
could cover four different facts through one relation or one passage and still pass.

Before the rerun, define a frozen concentration statistic and threshold, add it to the per-seed gate, and retain
duplicate-qid concentration separately. At minimum report normalized-cue duplicate share and source-passage
share; if relation/template labels or a frozen clustering rule are available, gate on their maximum share as
originally intended. Do not choose the threshold after seeing this run.

## Two corrections needed to avoid a false null

First, simplification 4 does not describe the code. `audit_arm` compares the candidate question or statement
against `gold question + gold answer` for **all** arms. For QA arms this is an asymmetric Jaccard comparison: the
candidate answer is excluded while answer tokens inflate the gold union. Use question-to-question signature
matching for QA arms and statement-to-question-plus-answer matching for declaratives. Save best and runner-up
scores so matcher misses can be audited. The 3B proposition judge remains the truth check; Jaccard is only a
recall-oriented prefilter.

Second, the source arm sees only the first 1,200 characters but is scored against facts from the whole passage.
In the repository census artifact, 228 of 355 passages (64.2%) exceed 1,200 characters; the median is 1,294 and
the maximum is 3,326. This can suppress the practical READ-step baseline for reasons unrelated to
self-annotation. Either chunk the full current passage with an honestly counted token budget, or freeze the
source arm's eligible manifest to propositions whose supporting span is visible while retaining the full common
manifest as the main cross-arm denominator. Token-based, sentence-boundary truncation is preferable to raw
character cutting.

## Cost and arm interpretation

The code calculates generated-token counts (`g1`, `g2`, `g4`, `g5`) but drops them from the result, and it does
not retain wall time or dual-checkpoint forward cost. Save those quantities per arm. Contrastive generation has
roughly two checkpoint forwards per generated step, whereas passive/source annotation has one; that difference
is central to deciding whether delta extraction beats simply annotating the source already in hand. Report total
candidate bytes and bytes per unique valid proposition, not just bytes for hidden-gold matches.

Simplification 3 is acceptable only as a **paired readout from one generation pass**. It is not two independent
generation arms, and its cost must not be counted twice. Also, because `qa2` retains a question only when the
subsequent answer parses, the present `contrast_q` readout is conditioned on successful full-QA formatting. Either
rename it accordingly or parse and audit question-only continuations independently if the scientific question
requires a true question-channel arm. Save `d_q` and `d_a` for all parseable QA candidates, not only hidden-gold
matches, so matcher selection cannot manufacture the channel result.

## What may be concluded after repair

A repaired A0 pass would show that a current source or immediate acquisition delta can autonomously construct a
reasonably clean, compact **training-time** replay ledger. That advances write-time self-annotation while keeping
the final inference artifact one dense, closed-book checkpoint. It would not yet show continual retention,
sublinear lifetime refresh cost, or a reason to grow.

Promotion still requires replaying the self-authored ledger across later unrelated book writes, deleting the
pre-write checkpoint and transient candidates, and recovering at least half of the gold-ledger retention gap at
matched replay tokens/FLOPs, with new-book acquisition and base/neutral drift within 0.03. Charge persistent bytes,
all replay tokens, annotation-generation FLOPs, and audit/controller FLOPs. This remains legal sequential
rehearsal rather than joint full-history retraining, and growth remains closed until it measurably bends the
lifetime refresh-demand curve.

Because this is a focused implementation review and launch ruling, I am not adding a new brainstorming branch.
