# Sanity check: finish the run, but keep the verdict exploratory

Source read: `qa/claude/2026-07-10.17.53.10.md`

## Bottom line

The rewrite is a substantial improvement and is worth letting finish. The raw rows, equivalent-only analysis,
semantic-destroying control, and frozen-base lift all move the probe toward the right mechanistic question.
However, neither of the two current calls is yet strong enough to choose the R50 branch:

1. A single binary judgment from the same 3B model that generated the paraphrases is not a sufficiently
   independent proposition-equivalence gate. Large lexical change is necessary for the **primary stress set**, but
   it is not evidence of semantic equivalence and should not replace a stronger audit.
2. The `0.5` and `0.25` bands are reasonable **descriptive regions**, but only for a per-fact paired retained-lift
   statistic. The code currently compares the median variant lift of failures with a pooled mean full lift, so the
   present label should remain an annotation rather than a decision rule.

Do not interrupt this run. Publish its distributions as a hardened scout, then correct the audit and paired
normalization before allowing `decoding_competition` or `encoding_surface_sensitive` to select training.

## Call (a): the 3B judge should not be the sole gate

Keep high-change audited paraphrases for the primary surface-sensitivity test, but retain near-copies as a separate
easy positive-control stratum rather than deleting them. Pooling near-copies with genuinely distant paraphrases can
indeed mask a narrow access basin: the median may look robust simply because most variants barely moved.

The more serious issue is correlated audit error. `_load_3b()` supplies the same model for paraphrase generation and
`judge_equiv`; the judge sees only two questions, emits binary `yes/no`, has no `ambiguous` state, receives neither
the intended gold answer nor the source proposition, and exposes no confidence in the saved row. A generator can
therefore approve its own relation drift, lost qualifier, argument reversal, or underspecified shortening. A
self-reported/logit confidence from that same pass would not solve the independence problem.

For the next decision-grade run, use this high-precision gate:

- Save content-token Jaccard/edit distance and analyze at least `near` and `far` equivalent strata. Pre-freeze the
  far cutoff from the generated-distance distribution or from a held-out calibration set; do not choose it after
  seeing gold-access outcomes.
- Give the auditor the original question, candidate, gold answer, and preferably the source fact. Ask whether both
  questions uniquely request the same subject-relation-object proposition; allow `equivalent`, `changed`, and
  `ambiguous`, with `ambiguous` excluded from the primary set.
- Require agreement across two order-swapped, differently worded audit passes, or use a different/stronger judge for
  the second pass. A small blinded human audit of accepted `far` examples is the cleanest calibration because this
  is offline measurement, not inference-time memory.
- Run the audit itself on known positives and on wrong-entity, wrong-relation, negation, qualifier-change, and
  argument-swap negatives. Freeze an acceptance target such as at least 95% rejection of known negatives and at
  least 95% acceptance of clean positives; otherwise mark the semantic gate invalid.
- Require at least 20 **unique facts per seed** in the far-equivalent failure set. The present count is rows, so two
  `para` variants of the same `qid` create pseudoreplication. Bootstrap by fact and report each variant family
  separately before pooling.

Thus the direct answer is: **yes, lexical distance should define the primary stress stratum, but no, “3B confident”
plus distance is not by itself a trustworthy equivalence gate**. Agreement and calibrated controls matter more than
the model claiming confidence.

## Call (b): keep 0.5/0.25 only as paired advisory bands

The qualitative bands make sense: retaining at least half of the acquired lift is evidence against complete access
collapse, while retaining at most a quarter is evidence toward collapse. The empty middle is useful because it
prevents borderline points from forcing a binary story. But the relevant statistic for fact `i` and variant `v` is

`retained_lift(i,v) = lift(i,v) / lift(i,full)`

with the paired difference `lift(i,v) - lift(i,full)` reported alongside it. Only include facts whose
`lift(i,full)` is clearly positive; ratios around zero or with negative denominators are unstable and cannot show
that acquisition added accessible knowledge. Prefer medians and fact-clustered intervals over a ratio of aggregate
statistics.

The current implementation instead compares:

- the median `lift` among all equivalent failed rows, to
- `full_lift_mean`, a mean over all answerable facts and then over accumulated seeds.

That mixes mean with median, failed with non-failed populations, variant families, facts, and potentially seeds.
It can label a low-baseline fact “collapsed” because other facts have high full lift, or label another “retained”
for the reverse reason. It also behaves nonsensically if pooled full lift is near zero or negative.

Use semantic controls to calibrate the bands after pairing. The far-equivalent distribution should be compared
with the near-equivalent positive control and the wrong-entity/wrong-relation negative distributions. Freeze the
bands, then apply them unchanged to a held-out seed. If `0.5/0.25` cleanly separates those controls, retain them;
otherwise let control quantiles define advisory regions. Do not tune cutoffs to make the desired mechanism win.

## Remaining implementation blockers before a branch verdict

The note says the four blockers are fixed, but the fourth is only partially fixed and several smaller issues still
matter:

- Rows need `variant_id`, original/full question, `gold_mlp_M_full`, `gold_mlp_base_full`, per-fact `full_lift`,
  lexical distance, and audit score/agreement. Both generated paraphrases currently share `variant="para"`, so
  `(seed,qid,variant)` is not unique. The summary currently reports medians, not the claimed quartiles.
- `classify()` pools all equivalent variant types and all seeds, and its `>=20` requirement is neither per seed nor
  per unique fact.
- The swap control is not a validity gate. `swap_fail` excludes swaps that incorrectly continue to return the gold,
  and the verdict is still emitted even if no swap fails. Evaluate all controls, require EM and retained lift to
  separate from equivalents, and return `invalid_control` when they do not.
- Validate that entity replacement actually changed the string and preserved the relation. `_entity_cue()` is a
  heuristic and `str.replace()` can silently produce a no-op or malformed question. Add a wrong-relation control as
  an orthogonal calibration.
- The `decoding_competition` predicate currently uses lift alone; it does not require `winner_is_base_lure`, despite
  the prose. Exact normalized equality between two greedy strings is also a narrow lure detector. The requested
  gold-prefix token ranks, first divergence margin, forced-prefix recovery, and stop-token-separated scores are
  still absent. High retained lift says the write signal survives the surface; it does not by itself prove that a
  small decoding competition caused the wrong answer.
- `drop_from_full` measures the updated model's surface change, not acquisition-specific lift loss. Base-model gold
  probability can itself vary across paraphrases, so the paired full-versus-variant lift is the primary access
  quantity; retain `drop_from_full` only as a complementary observation.

A decision-grade classification should therefore require all three conditions:

1. semantic audit and negative controls pass;
2. paired retained-lift behavior is stable per seed and variant family; and
3. token-level competition evidence supports the decode claim, rather than lift alone.

## Project-level ruling

This remains an offline read-path auditor, not evidence of continual retention, rehearsal-free learning, or a need
for growth. Its legitimate role is to choose the next **training-time** mechanism. If competition is confirmed,
train the gold-over-lure margin into the updated weights and discard both base and judge before ordinary greedy
evaluation. If surface collapse is confirmed, train structured multiview addressing into the same checkpoint.
Either route must later beat a token/step-matched no-replay control on held-out access and later-stream retention,
preserve newest and neutral/base capabilities, use one checkpoint with no external inference memory, and avoid
full-history joint retraining. Nothing in this probe licenses growth; growth still requires fixed-capacity
saturation plus a matched-compute advantage over large-from-start.
