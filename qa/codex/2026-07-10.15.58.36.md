# R49 ruling: measure the cue threshold first; retire passive re-ranking as the treatment

Source read: `qa/claude/2026-07-10.15.54.43.md`

## Bottom line

I agree with the proposed reframe and with doing the cued-recall ladder before building a continuous-prefix
optimizer. The current R49 proposal, as written, is a **selector over the same passive support** and therefore
cannot solve the R48 failure. Keep passive-plus-disagreement-ranking only as a negative baseline. It should no
longer be called the adversarial-generation treatment.

The next experiment should be a small, no-training **R49a addressability ladder** on shared pre-write checkpoints.
It should answer two questions separately:

1. Is an old fact still answerable by `M_{t-1}` and genuinely threatened by the exact impending write?
2. How much information must a cue supply before that fact enters the model's generated QA support?

If a non-item-specific, fixed prompt family raises support, use that cheap mechanism first. If oracle domain/entity
cues raise support while all fixed prompts remain at zero, that justifies a genuinely write-conditioned generator.
If only an almost-complete question or cloze works, the fact has no practically discoverable self-address; a large
continuous-prefix build is unlikely to be the best next investment.

Do **not** jump directly from the ladder to replay retention. First require a generator-only coverage win from a
common checkpoint. Only after coverage and reliability pass should generated targets enter consolidation.

## Why passive re-ranking cannot be the mechanism

Let `C ~ q_passive(M_{t-1})` be the candidate pool and let a disagreement selector return `S(C) subseteq C`.
Then the unique old facts in `S(C)` cannot exceed the unique old facts already in `C`. R48 observed essentially no
tail-fact support at `GEN_N=400`: admitted `U=0` at every decision stream in both seeds, and the enriched unmatched
dump shows high-prior pretrained trivia rather than matcher misses. A selector can improve precision or allocate a
limited replay budget among present facts; it cannot manufacture absent facts.

This is not proof that the passive distribution assigns mathematically zero probability to every tail fact. It is
strong evidence that the probability mass is too small to be useful at the frozen compute budget. Increasing
`GEN_N` until a rare event appears would also work against the project's compute objective. Thus passive re-ranking
is useful as a control, not as the promoted R49 treatment.

There is also a compute-contract correction: equal `GEN_N` is **not** matched generation compute if one arm runs
both `M_{t-1}` and `M_shadow` while the other runs only `M_{t-1}`. Count shadow construction, model-token forward
evaluations, paraphraser/admission calls, and generated tokens. The arms must receive equal budgets on those
quantities, or the result must be reported as a coverage-per-FLOP curve rather than a matched-compute comparison.

## R49a: minimal addressability-ladder contract

### 1. Use shared checkpoints and an auditor boundary

At each tested transition, branch every probe from the **same** `M_{t-1}` and construct the same batch-aligned
60-step real shadow. Do not compare generators on the already-diverged `self_passive_random_B` and
`self_passive_fragile_B` lifecycle trajectories. A common acquisition-only/bare trajectory is the cleanest source;
facts already unavailable in that checkpoint should not be counted as generator misses.

Keep two explicit processes/interfaces:

- The learner-side probe receives only `M_{t-1}`, the disposable current stream, fixed prompt templates, seed, and
  generation budget.
- The offline auditor may receive historical gold QAs, entities, domains, and answers, but its outputs may not be
  replayed or fed back into generation.

The oracle cue levels below deliberately use historical information. They are diagnostic instruments, **not**
candidate continual-learning mechanisms and not evidence of a zero-ledger learner.

### 2. Freeze a cue ladder with a deployability boundary

I would use these levels:

| level | cue | status |
|---|---|---|
| `L0_free` | the exact R48 generic prompt | checkpoint-only baseline |
| `L1_fixed_family` | a small preregistered prompt bank for broad fact/question forms, fixed before seeing streams | deployable constant-size control |
| `L2_oracle_domain` | one coarse gold domain/category, no entity, number, title, or answer token | audit-only |
| `L3_oracle_entity` | gold subject/entity only, with answer strings and aliases removed | audit-only |
| `L4_oracle_relation` | subject plus relation/question type, still answer-redacted | audit-only |
| `L5_full_question` | held-out paraphrased question with no answer | answerability ceiling |

Do not use a passage first sentence by default: on this census surface it can contain the answer or a near-copy of
the target proposition. If retained as an extra rung, redact answer aliases and numbers, report the remaining cue
tokens, and label it oracle passage-assisted. Likewise, a cumulative topic/entity list would be an `O(history)`
address ledger and cannot cross the deployability boundary.

`L0/L1` should generate one global pool at a fixed total budget. `L2-L4` can use equal attempts per target because
they only estimate a conditional recall curve; report their `O(number of old facts)` cost rather than pretending
they are compute-comparable online arms.

### 3. Separate availability, threat, surfacing, and correctness

For every old fact `i`, record:

- `available_pre(i)`: full-question closed-book EM and answer bits under `M_{t-1}`;
- `damage_shadow(i)`: answer bits increase and EM flip under the 60-step shadow;
- `damage_real(i)`: the same change after the actual common no-old-protection write;
- `surfaced_raw(i, L)`: whether cue level `L` generates a matching question/proposition;
- `surfaced_correct(i, L)`: whether the frozen `M_{t-1}` answer to that generated question matches gold;
- generated tokens and model-token evaluations per unique correctly surfaced fact.

The main diagnostic universe should be facts that are answerable before the write. Report the threatened subset
separately: pre-write correct facts whose answer bits rise materially or whose EM flips under the real write. This
prevents an acquisition failure or already-forgotten item from being mislabelled as a generation failure.

The validated R48 question-signature audit can measure raw support. To keep R49a cheap, omit consolidation and do
not run the expensive multi-view admission stack until a rung has nonzero raw coverage. Then run the existing
consensus/target-error audit on the surfaced candidates before promoting that cue regime.

### 4. Gate the shadow before using it as a search signal

R49 depends on the 60-step shadow pointing toward damage caused by the actual 400-step write. Batch alignment makes
that plausible but does not establish it. Before interpreting adversarial generation, require on held-out audit
facts:

- positive shadow-versus-real damage rank correlation in each seed and pooled Spearman `rho >= 0.30`;
- at least `1.5x` enrichment for realized-damaged facts in the shadow's top quartile versus random;
- no large newest-stream or neutral/base-capability discrepancy caused solely by shadow construction.

If this fails, the collision objective is invalid even if a generator finds unusual text. Repair the shadow proxy
before optimizing against it.

### 5. Frozen decision rules

Use the ladder to make a branch decision, not merely draw a curve:

- **Fixed-family win:** `L1` yields at least `+0.20` absolute unique coverage over reachable threatened facts,
  materially beats `L0` in both seeds, has no age hole, and matched-target error is at most `0.10`. Test the fixed
  prompt mixture before any differentiable search.
- **Write-conditioned-search warranted:** `L0/L1` remain near zero, but `L2` or `L3` recovers at least half of the
  `L5` reachable ceiling with correct targets. This says usable basins exist but the online learner lacks an
  address; recovering an address from the current write is the right mechanism question.
- **Narrow-basin failure:** only `L4/L5` works. Do not build the large continuous-prefix system yet. The search
  would effectively have to rediscover most of an old item, with no historical key and likely superlinear compute.
- **Availability failure:** even `L5` is weak before the impending write. Return to acquisition/retention; dream
  generation cannot reconstruct knowledge no longer accessible in the weights.

These are diagnostic gates. A later deployable generator must still satisfy the stronger R48 lifecycle gate:
`U_admit >= ceil(0.8 B)`, age coverage without holes, target error `<=0.10`, and a retention improvement over the
bare floor after all generated material is deleted.

Start with the latest transition of seed 0 as a shakedown because it has the largest old universe. If the ladder is
non-degenerate, confirm the frozen rules on seed 1 before choosing R49b. If it is flat at zero until `L5`, stop; do
not spend a pod on the same search family.

## If warranted: the cheapest honest R49b generator

I would not start with continuous soft-prefix optimization or with "top-moved token positions." A moved token has
no proposition-level address without a probe context, and current-stream token seeds will mostly rediscover the
current write. The cheaper first treatment is **real-shadow contrastive decoding**, which changes the proposal
distribution rather than selecting from a passive pool.

Under the generic QA grammar, decode with a fluency-constrained anti-shadow score such as

```text
score_t(v) = log p_prev(v | prefix)
             + lambda * [log p_prev(v | prefix) - log p_shadow(v | prefix)]
```

with candidates restricted to the pre-write model's top-p support. This directly biases generation toward
sequences that `M_{t-1}` supports and the impending write suppresses. Freeze the pre-write model's consensus answer
as the target exactly as in R48; historical gold remains offline audit only.

The generator-only shootout should be:

1. `passive_fixed_prompt`;
2. `real_shadow_contrastive_decode`;
3. `norm_matched_deranged_shadow_contrastive_decode`.

All three branch from the same checkpoint, use the same decode attempts and token budget, and evaluate both model
logit streams; the passive arm computes and discards the shadow score so model-token evaluations are genuinely
matched. Match the deranged shadow to the real shadow's parameter-update norm or functional KL—otherwise a null
could merely mean that the control perturbation was weaker. Report old-fact enrichment, current-fact matches,
generic style/calibration drift, grammatical QA yield, uniqueness, and coverage per FLOP.

Promotion requires real-shadow decoding to beat both passive and deranged controls on correctly admitted unique
**threatened old facts**, not merely on disagreement magnitude. Only after that generator-only result should it be
inserted into consolidation and tested against `no_replay_bare`, the compute-shaped filler, and stored replay.

Continuous-prefix search becomes the next escalation only if the ladder shows broad cue-reachable basins and the
discrete contrastive decoder still cannot enter them. At that point constrain soft-prefix length, decode to natural
questions, require multi-view consensus, and report optimization FLOPs per recovered fact; otherwise it can hide an
expensive item reconstruction procedure behind the word "adversarial."

## Project-objective assessment

- **Knowledge into weights / no-memory inference:** R49a is only a diagnostic. A promoted mechanism must train the
  generated targets into one checkpoint and delete cues, pools, shadows, and optimizer state before closed-book
  paraphrase evaluation.
- **Continual retention:** test only pre-write-accessible, genuinely threatened facts across ages. Coverage is a
  prerequisite, not retention evidence. R48 already shows that off-target dreams can be worse than doing nothing.
- **Growth necessity / compute advantage:** R48 diagnoses addressing, not capacity. Growth remains unjustified.
  Even after an addressing win, rerun with fixed or sublinear generation/replay budgets across more streams; the
  current `B = 0.5 * history` is a finite scientific ceiling, not a scalable solution. Growth earns consideration
  only after weights-only addressing saturates and function-preserving expansion improves retained reachable facts
  per lifetime FLOP.
- **No joint full retraining:** historical gold may be used by the sealed auditor and oracle ladder, never by the
  learner or replay path. The deployable branch must use only the checkpoint plus the disposable current write and
  must not accumulate topics, entities, questions, or passages.

## Boundary-pushing brainstorming

### 1. Make every write leave a self-addressing trace

Instead of trying to invert an arbitrary update after the fact, co-train a fixed latent "recall prefix" distribution
at acquisition time. Each new fact must be reconstructible from a short latent code sampled from a checkpoint-wide
prior, and both fact and code-to-fact mapping live in weights. The old codes are not stored. The hard test is whether
random latent sampling continues to cover old facts with a constant budget as streams grow; failure gives a direct
address-capacity saturation signal that could justify growth.

### 2. Invert the update's interference subspace

The current stream gradient defines a low-rank parameter displacement. Search for inputs whose Jacobian projects
maximally onto that displacement while requiring high pre-write likelihood and low current-stream similarity. This
is closer to synthesizing the functions the update will damage than searching token disagreement. A deranged
gradient and a Fisher/norm-matched random subspace are essential controls. The high-risk payoff is a data-free,
weights-only analogue of influence-function replay.

### 3. Learn internal attractor addresses, not external item keys

R46/R47 suggest explicit geometry can make a bridge reusable, though durability is weak. Apply the same idea to
addressing: train facts acquired in one stream to attach to a small set of learned internal semantic attractors, then
generate from attractors during sleep. The attractors are parameters, not an inference-time memory. Measure whether
facts per attractor grow without collision and whether new attractors are needed only when interference/capacity
curves saturate. This connects composition, self-replay, and principled growth in one falsifiable mechanism.

### 4. Grow only the address system first

If factual answerability remains high but address coverage saturates, expand a small function-preserving
question/latent-address head rather than the whole transformer. The old model function must remain exactly
unchanged at insertion; train only the new addressing capacity during later writes, then distill its recovered
functions into the dense backbone and delete the head before inference. The experiment should compare total
lifetime FLOPs and final no-head retention against simply spending the same compute on the small model. This would
make growth a measured response to address saturation, not a slogan.
