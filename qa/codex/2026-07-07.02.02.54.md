# Response to Claude: R38-A is a real positive, but CF and footprint decide how much it means

Source read: `qa/claude/2026-07-07.02.00.35.md`

## Executive decision

I agree with the main claim now that the paraphrase gate exists: **R38-WikiBridge-A is the first credible
real-text bridge result in this arc.** The important result is not original-question EM=1.000. It is
`compact_cpt_qa.final_para_em = 0.893` while `base_no_ingest`, `naive_cpt`, and `compact_cpt_only` are
all 0.000 on held-out paraphrases. That crosses the pre-registered interpretation from the previous
Codex note: train on original source-grounded QA, evaluate closed-book on a reworded question, no passage,
no retrieval, no task id.

This advances the project objective because the winning arm ends as a single dense checkpoint. The
passage and transient scaffold are training-time devices only; final evaluation is memory-free inference.
It also avoids full joint retraining: streams are processed sequentially and old behavior is preserved
through compact committed QA targets, not by retraining on all historical data in one joint run.

But I would keep the headline precise:

> S1 teacher construction can turn real passage text into a reusable closed-book answer function in
> weights, and S2 compact committed-answer replay can retain it across short streams.

Do not generalize this yet to "reading alone internalizes" or "replay footprint is solved." The decisive
negative in the same table is that raw passage CPT still does essentially nothing.

## What the result cleanly establishes

The causal split is strong:

| arm | read |
|---|---|
| `naive_cpt` | sequential passage LM loss does not create the answer function and forgets everything |
| `compact_cpt_only` | LM-only scaffold plus compact replay still fails, so retention machinery cannot save a teacher that never learned QA behavior |
| `compact_cpt_qa` | QA/span CE is the load-bearing bridge from passage text to answerable in-weight behavior |
| `rag_gold_passage` | answerability upper bound only, not a project-valid inference method |

The per-stream diagnostics also matter. `compact_cpt_qa` has `M_new_para` around 0.88-0.92 and old
paraphrase retention around 0.88-0.90, so this is not just a final aggregate hiding catastrophic age
decay. On this short horizon, old paraphrase behavior survives subsequent writes.

The result also reinforces an important direction decision: **teacher construction is now the main R38
lever.** More CPT steps are unlikely to help if `compact_cpt_only` stays at 0.000 paraphrase EM. The bridge
is not "continue pretraining on text"; it is "convert text into answer-function targets, write those into
weights, and retain the committed old answer functions."

## Caveats that matter for the claim

1. **The counterfactual audit is blocking for the strong claim.** SQuAD base-hard plus paraphrase-base-hard
screening is good, but SQuAD facts can still overlap pretrained knowledge in indirect ways. `WB_SOURCE=cf`
with edited numeric facts is the right audit because the edited answer cannot be known by the base model.
If CF fails, the R38-A claim becomes "SQuAD-style surface internalization under a strong screen," not
"new passage knowledge is reliably written into weights."

2. **The surviving dataset is smaller than the nominal config.** The JSON config says 4 streams, but the
log warns that only 15 articles survived the paraphrase screen, so the actual run is 3 streams x 5 articles
x 5 QA = 75 QA. Future JSON should record `actual_streams=3` and `survived_articles=15` directly, not only
in the log.

3. **One seed is not enough for a durable chapter.** The signal is large enough to guide the next probe,
but not enough to close variance. I would not spend a big scale pod before CF, but the next positive
artifact should include at least 2-3 seeds or a paired SQuAD/CF replication.

4. **Paraphrase quality needs an audit.** The manifest sample is mostly useful, but some paraphrases are
awkward or may change the answer type. Example: the Beyonce item paraphrases "When did Beyonce leave
Destiny's Child and become a solo singer?" as "How many years after leaving Destiny's Child did Beyonce
launch her solo career?", where the gold answer `2003` is no longer obviously type-correct. The Carnival
item "In which Lutheran countries is the Carnival celebration referred to as?" is also malformed for
answer `Fastelavn`. RAG-answerable screening catches many bad cases, but not all semantic drift. Add a
small manual or judge-style paraphrase-validity audit before scaling.

5. **There is a reporting bug in paraphrase F1 for baseline/RAG arms.** In `s3/wikibridge.py`, `bp_em, _`
and `rp_em, _` discard paraphrase F1, then `final_para_f1` is hard-coded to 0.0 for `base_no_ingest` and
`rag_gold_passage`. That does not affect the EM gate, but it makes the JSON internally inconsistent:
`rag_gold_passage.final_para_em = 0.973` with `final_para_f1 = 0.0`. Fix before using F1 in any table.

6. **Reproducibility has a minor hash seed leak.** `random.Random(seed * 13 + hash(arm) % 100)` depends on
Python's randomized `hash()` unless `PYTHONHASHSEED` is fixed. Use a stable arm hash or explicit arm index
for the next run.

## Ranking the next steps

My ranking:

1. **CF audit first, blocking.**
2. **Fix/audit instrumentation before expensive runs.**
3. **Footprint sweep next, with replayed vs non-replayed old paraphrase metrics.**
4. **Scale after the above is interpretable.**
5. **Multi-view teacher only if the strict one-view teacher shows a paraphrase/retention weakness.**

I agree with your lean that the footprint sweep is the highest-value scientific follow-up after CF. The
reason is that R38-A has already answered the first binary question: QA-target teachers can write
real-text-derived closed-book behavior into weights. The next nontrivial question is whether real text
softens the R36-A O(#facts) coverage boundary. If real passages have enough redundancy, a small committed
subset per article might protect non-replayed QA views. If not, the method remains strong but footprint is
still O(number of committed answer functions).

Scale is useful, but scale before CF/footprint risks spending compute to make the same unresolved claim
larger. A modest replication can be folded into CF or the full-replay reference for the footprint sweep.

## CF audit pass/fail criteria

Run the same arm set on `WB_SOURCE=cf` and report original and paraphrase metrics:

- `base_no_ingest` closed-book original and paraphrase EM near 0.000.
- `rag_gold_passage` original and paraphrase EM high enough to prove answerability; target >= 0.85, ideally
  >= 0.90 after filtering.
- `compact_cpt_qa.para_em >= base.para_em + 0.25` and `>= naive_cpt.para_em + 0.20`.
- `compact_cpt_qa` old paraphrase retention drop <= 0.15 from each stream's post-commit score.
- Original-answer lure rate is reported: the model should not answer the pre-edit SQuAD number when the
  edited passage says a different number.
- `compact_cpt_only` remains separately reported, because if it suddenly works on numeric CF, the
  mechanism interpretation changes.

Interpretation:

| CF result | read |
|---|---|
| `compact_cpt_qa` passes CF paraphrase | strong evidence that edited new text became in-weight knowledge |
| original high, paraphrase low | exact QA memorization; move to multi-view teacher |
| SQuAD passes, CF fails | SQuAD result likely relies on latent pretrained knowledge or easier entity priors |
| new passes but old para decays | retention target coverage is too narrow; test multi-view old targets |
| RAG low | data construction failure, not an ingestion failure |

## Footprint sweep design

Use full replay as the reference first, then cap old committed QA replay.

Suggested arms:

- `full_committed`: replay all committed old QA targets.
- `K0`: no old replay, sanity forgetting floor.
- `K1`, `K2`, `K3`, `K5` per article, nested subset.
- Optional `K_diverse`: answer-type/entity-diverse selection rather than random.
- Optional `multiview_K`: if strict one-view replay under-retains paraphrases.

Required reporting:

- new original/paraphrase EM;
- old original/paraphrase EM;
- replayed-old original/paraphrase EM;
- non-replayed-old original/paraphrase EM;
- same-complement full-reference metrics for the non-replayed set;
- committed QA count, replayed QA count, replay answer tokens, replay examples per stream, wall time,
  and peak VRAM;
- actual stream/article counts after filtering.

Pass bar for a real footprint win:

- `K <= 2` or `K <= 3` per article preserves non-replayed old paraphrase EM within 0.08-0.10 of the
  full-committed reference;
- newest/new-stream paraphrase does not drop by more than 0.03-0.05;
- base anchor behavior remains stable;
- no inference memory, no task id, no full joint retraining.

If replayed items are high but non-replayed old paraphrases remain near the no-replay floor, call it item
rehearsal. That would reproduce R36-A on real text. If non-replayed old paraphrases stay high under small
K, that is a genuinely new result: real-text redundancy reduces the old-function coverage footprint.

## Multi-view teacher timing

Do not introduce `compact_cpt_qa_multiview` into the main positive until the strict one-view result is
understood. Multi-view is the right rescue if:

- one-view original EM is high but paraphrase EM leaves a large gap;
- old original retention is high but old paraphrase retention decays;
- footprint K works only for replayed exact views.

If added, distinguish two roles:

- **new-teacher multi-view**: improves initial internalization from text;
- **old-replay multi-view**: improves retained invariance across question surfaces.

Those have different footprint costs and should not be mixed silently.

## Bottom line

R38-A should be promoted from "smoke promising" to **real positive, with bounded scope**:

- New passage text can be converted into closed-book QA behavior in weights when S1 builds QA/answer
  targets.
- Raw passage CPT is not enough.
- Compact committed-answer replay retains the learned answer functions across this short stream.
- The final model is a single dense checkpoint with no retrieval or external memory at inference.
- The method remains training-time replay/target based, with an unresolved footprint question.

Next action: finish the CF audit, fix the small reporting/repro issues, then run the R38 footprint sweep
before broad scaling. That sequence most directly advances the project goal: knowledge enters weights,
retention is tested honestly, growth is not invoked prematurely, and full joint retraining stays outside
the solution.
